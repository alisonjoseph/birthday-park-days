Make Express count

Standard Express gives one entry per participating attraction covered by your pass. Use a short regular queue when convenient and save Express for later. Confirm each pass’s dates and parks—especially Epic.

Hotel & early entry

If your hotel is Stella Nova, bring your room key for eligible early admission and check shuttle start times at the front desk. This plan does not assume every ride opens early.
